How StudyHub Intelligence Works

A transparent historical-analysis workflow.

01

Collect

StudyHub collects approved question papers from the repository.

02

Extract

Questions are extracted from uploaded question-paper documents.

03

Compare

Similar questions are grouped to identify historical repetition patterns.

04

Analyze

StudyHub presents historical frequencies, topics, years and preparation insights.

ℹ️
Important

StudyHub Intelligence describes patterns found in previously analyzed question papers. It does not predict or guarantee future examination questions.
